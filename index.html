Tracing biological circuits in single-cell foundation models

First author · Manuscript available

Mechanistic interpretability · Single-cell genomics

  • Sparse transcoders trained across all 12 MLP layers on approximately 4.3 million cell-gene tokens.

Biology-guided de novo peptide sequencing

Ongoing research · University of Michigan

Computational proteomics · Structured prediction

A Casanovo-style spectrum-to-sequence Transformer that incorporates fragmentation biology through mass-aware attention and training-time privileged distillation.

  • Adds a per-head attention bias that combines learned pairwise peak features with chemical priors centered on amino-acid and modification masses.
  • Uses a frozen teacher with ground-truth-restored hybrid spectra to supervise an observed-spectrum student across all decoder positions; inference uses the student alone.
  • Across nine leave-one-species-out benchmarks, attention bias, privileged distillation, and their combination improve average accuracy by 3.6%, 2.0%, and 4.6%, respectively.
