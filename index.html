Featured research

Current projects at the intersection of machine learning and biomedical discovery. Project status is stated explicitly for work in progress.

01

Mechanistic interpretability · Single-cell genomics

Tracing biological circuits inside single-cell foundation models

A post-norm transcoder framework for studying how scGPT transforms gene-level signals into higher-order biological programs.

  • Sparse transcoders across all 12 MLP layers and approximately 4.3M cell-gene tokens
  • Layerwise transition from single-gene detectors to multi-gene biological programs
  • Glutamatergic, synaptic, and oligodendrocyte-maturation circuits examined with activation patching
First-author manuscript in preparation
02

Computational pathology · Self-supervised learning

Cellular guidance for histopathology representation learning

A pathology-specific learning framework that uses self-derived cellular composition to guide the teacher while keeping the student image-only at inference.

  • Whole-slide and region-level histopathology representation learning
  • Cellular histograms as biologically grounded supervisory structure
  • Evaluation across scanners, tissue domains, and downstream transfer tasks
Ongoing research · Mayo Clinic KIMIA Lab
03

Computational proteomics · Structured prediction

Graph-structured, mass-aware de novo peptide sequencing

A spectrum-to-sequence Transformer that injects sparse mass relationships into spectral representations and decoding.

  • Graph-structured mass information for MS/MS representation learning
  • Missing-peak imputation and mass-constrained inference
  • Held-out-species evaluation and systematic component ablations
Ongoing research · University of Michigan
